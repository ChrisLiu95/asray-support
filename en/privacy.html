Privacy policy

01Accounts and conversations

Account details, passwords and invitation codes support registration and sign-in. Passwords are stored as salted hashes on the server; session tokens use system secure storage in the app. Messages you send, language, service preferences and conversation records support concierge replies and history. Do not send unnecessary identity, financial, health or third-party sensitive information.

02Images, location and speech

Images you choose or take and send are re-encoded, resized and stripped of metadata before recognition; information visible in the pixels remains visible. Current image recognition runs on the service backend, not through DeepSeek. Nearby searches send location for the requested search only after you grant permission; the app does not continuously track background location. System speech recognition produces an unsent text draft; tapping Send submits the text. The app does not store recordings or upload audio to our service. System speech processing may use the network.

03Applications and device copies

Applications include name, company, job title, email, phone, requested service, language and status, linked to your account. We use them to process requests and contact you; an application is not a marketing opt-in. Necessary service staff may access the data; any required transfer to a supplier should be separately explained. The app stores preferences such as language and currency, but does not persist chat text as ordinary local preferences. You manage invitation copies saved to Photos.

04AI and service providers

AI text replies use the official DeepSeek API. The backend may send your request text, conversation state, retrieved service information and context. Avoid unnecessary personal data. Provider processing follows the actual service terms and policies; we do not promise zero retention or exclusion from training. See the DeepSeek Privacy Policy. The backend is hosted on Microsoft Azure; see the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Processing may occur outside your location. Contact us for recipient and cross-border processing information.

05Permissions and choices

Manage camera, photos, microphone, speech and location permissions in system settings. App versions with the new permission feature explain DeepSeek and the shared information before the first AI consultation; sending requires tapping Agree and continue. If you decline, you can still browse categories and information and submit invitation applications. Permission lasts only for the current signed-in session and is requested again after sign-out or restart. Withdraw it through AI data permission in the sidebar to block future AI requests and cancel requests still waiting. Data already sent cannot be recalled and is handled under this policy. Update older versions without this screen before using AI consultations. Hiding a conversation changes only the device list, not server records.

06Retention, deletion and security

Accounts, conversations and applications are retained while you use the service; there is currently no unified fixed-day automatic deletion schedule. You may request deletion. In newer app versions supporting deletion, open the account drawer → Delete account, enter your current password and confirm. Only server-confirmed success means online account data was deleted. If the entry is missing or unavailable, email us so we can verify and process your request and explain its status. Signing out or uninstalling is not account deletion.

07Deletion scope and exceptions

Deletion covers the online account, sign-ins, conversations, invitations and schedule, but not copies you saved to Photos. Backups, operational/security logs and information legally required to be retained may not be erased at the same time. We will explain the applicable scope, retention reasons and handling arrangements for your request. Data already processed by third parties is also subject to their actual terms. HTTPS and secure storage reduce risk; they do not provide end-to-end encryption or absolute security.

08Rights, minors and changes

Contact the email below for access, correction, copies, deletion, withdrawal or privacy complaints. We may request information necessary to verify identity; never send passwords or tokens. The service is not directed at children. Minors should use it under guardian guidance and must not enter transactions beyond their legal capacity. Contact us about children's data. Changes appear here, with separate notice or permission where required.

09This website

These pages use GitHub Pages, require no sign-in and contain no advertising, analytics scripts or form tracking deployed by us. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security; see GitHub Pages documentation. Email links open your email service, whose own rules apply.
